# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-07_

Petty does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It makes no network requests, has no analytics, and has no account.

## What Petty stores

Petty saves a small amount of data with Chrome's own storage, on your device:

| Data                                       | Where                     | Why                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Which pets you added (an id and a species) | `chrome.storage.sync`     | So your pets come back after a restart.                                                 |
| Each pet's last position and animation     | `chrome.storage.session`  | So pets continue where they were when you switch tabs. Cleared when the browser closes. |
| Your theme choice (light, auto or dark)    | The popup's local storage | So the popup opens in the theme you picked.                                             |

If Chrome Sync is turned on, Chrome syncs `chrome.storage.sync` data across your devices through your Google account. Petty itself never sees or sends it.

## Web pages

Petty adds its pets to the web pages you visit. It does not read page content, form fields, browsing history, or cookies. The only page information it uses is the window size, to keep pets on screen.

## Removing your data

Click **Clear all** in the popup, or remove the extension. Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.
