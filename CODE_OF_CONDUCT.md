# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Petty to be a friendly and welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainer, [@shanto462](https://github.com/shanto462). You can also use GitHub's [Report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. All reports are handled privately and fairly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful. Responses range from a private warning to a permanent ban, depending on how serious the behavior is.
