A quiet afternoon on the web

5 min read

Some pages are made for skimming and some are made for staying. This one is made for staying. Pour something warm, lean back, and let the small creatures at the bottom of the screen keep you company while you read.

They walk to the edge, turn around, and walk back. Now and then one of them stops to eat, to stretch, or to fall asleep in a sunny spot. Pick one up and drop it somewhere else if you like. It will land on its feet.

Open another tab and they are already there, right where you left them. Close the browser and they will be waiting when you come back.
